Health Examination

Health Examination Information

In accordance with the Occupational Safety and Health Act, please complete a health examination at a regional-level (or higher) hospital or one of the recommended hospitals before your start date.

📋What to Bring

  • Original passport or ARC (and NHI card, if available)
  • One 2-inch or 1-inch ID photo taken within 6 months
  • Examination fee (self-paid)

🏥Hospital Requirements

Clinic reports are not accepted. Please choose a regional-level (or higher) hospital or one listed below; partner hospitals are recommended for discounted rates.

⏰Already Have a Report?

If you already have a report issued within the last 3 months by a regional-level (or higher) hospital, you do not need another examination.

The Longtan branch does not offer special examination items — please use the Jingguo branch or another recommended hospital if needed.
  • Te-Jen Hospital: NT$700, general examination only.
  • Hua Yang Hospital: NT$580, general examination only.

Non-partner hospitals: general examination about NT$850 per person (subject to each hospital's current rates); special examination items about NT$250–650 depending on the item.

Before Your Examination & Examination Procedure

Pre-examination notes (conducted in accordance with the Occupational Safety and Health Act):

  • For 3 days before the exam, avoid staying up late, alcohol, stimulants, strong tea or coffee, raw meat, and seafood.
  • Wear light clothing; avoid underwear with buttons or metal, necklaces, and items in your shirt pocket, as they interfere with the X-ray.
  • Fast from 20:00 the night before (no food or water) — at least 6–8 hours of fasting — to avoid affecting test results.
  • If you wear glasses, bring them for the vision test.
  • If you are or may be pregnant, please inform the registration desk and do not take the X-ray.
  • For the urine test, collect midstream urine in the plastic cup, fill the test tube about 80%, and hand it to the nurse. If you are on your period or it ended less than a week ago, please tell the nurse.
  • Rest for 5 minutes and relax before your blood pressure is taken.
  • Relax during the blood draw; afterwards, press firmly on the site for 5 minutes and do not rub it.
  • During the hearing test, sounds go from loud to soft; use your finger to indicate left or right to the nurse.

Examination procedure: 01 Registration → 02 Height & weight → 03 Blood pressure → 04 Vision & color blindness → 05 Blood draw → 06 Hearing → 07 Urine → 08 X-ray → 09 Physician consultation → 10 Submit forms & finish

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

If you have any questions, check the answers below or contact the HR contact in your offer letter.

What if my health report has not arrived before Day 1?

If you were examined at a partner hospital, you can check in with the examination receipt first. Otherwise, keep your proof of examination and let HR know in advance to confirm how to proceed.

What if I do not have a CTBC or Mega Bank account?

Bring the first page of your offer letter to the bank to open one; please confirm the required documents and processing time with the bank first.

Can I still report if some documents are not ready?

Please tell HR before Day 1 which documents are missing and when you can provide them; HR will help confirm how to proceed.

Can I drive straight into the plant on Day 1?

Parking spaces require prior application. Please park according to your plant's instructions on this page and HR's notice; once you have your parking permit, you can enter as regulated.

What if I am delayed or need accessibility assistance?

Please contact the HR recruiter in your offer letter as early as possible with your estimated arrival time or the assistance you need, so we can arrange your reception.

Have you filled in your Employee Information?

To set up your access in advance, please complete it at least 7 days before your start date.
